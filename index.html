Welcome

Jiahua Zhu

I am Jiahua, currently working as an Assistant Professor in Finance in King's Business School at King's College London. Before joining Kings, I worked as an Assistant Professor at University of Essex, and Tianjin Univeristy.

I received a PhD ​ in Economics in the School of Social Sciences at Nanyang Technological University (NTU), Singapore, in October 2020.

My research interest is behavioral and experimental economics, with a specific interest in experimental finance. I am now also working on digitial finance in experiments, LLM and ML in Economics, etc.
